Frequently asked questions

How can I schedule a test drive?

Call us at +94 77 123 4567, send us a message through this form, or WhatsApp us directly. We'll arrange a convenient time for your test drive.

What's your response time?

We typically respond to emails within 24 hours. Phone calls during business hours are answered immediately. WhatsApp messages receive responses within a few hours.

Can I visit without an appointment?

Yes! We're open Monday-Saturday 8am-6pm. While walk-ins are welcome, calling ahead ensures our team is available to give you their full attention.

Do you offer home inspections?

For vehicles in our inventory, inspections happen at our lot. However, we can discuss delivery options for customers in nearby areas.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, cash payments, and financing through partner banks. We can also arrange installment payment plans.

Do you have parking at the lot?

Yes! Ample parking available for customer vehicles. Easy access to the showroom and all our facilities.
