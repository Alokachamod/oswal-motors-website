Our story

How it all started

In 2004, Ravi Oswal opened the doors of Oswal Motors with a simple mission: to provide honest, transparent vehicle sales backed by thorough inspections. What started as a small lot in Gampola has grown into one of the most trusted dealerships in Central Province.

For over two decades, we've built our reputation on three pillars: Integrity in every transaction, Quality in every vehicle, and Care for every customer. Every car, SUV, and bike that leaves our lot has been meticulously inspected and prepared to ensure it meets our high standards.

Today, with more than 1,000 satisfied customers and over 500 successful transactions annually, Oswal Motors continues to be the go-to destination for families and individuals seeking reliable, inspected vehicles at fair prices.
